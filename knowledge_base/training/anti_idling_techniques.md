# Anti-Idling & Fuel Conservation Guidelines
*Demo / Synthetic Operating Reference - Advisory Only*

## Idling Thresholds & Environmental Impact
1. Unnecessary idling burns 2.5 to 4.0 liters of diesel per hour without generating work output.
2. If waiting for haul trucks exceeds 3 minutes, enable Caterpillar Auto-Idle system to reduce engine speed to 1,000 RPM.
3. For idle periods exceeding 7 minutes, safely park on level ground, lower implements, and shut down the engine.
4. Continuous idling beyond 30 minutes triggers automated fuel-efficiency advisories.
