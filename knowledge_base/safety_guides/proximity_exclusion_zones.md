# Proximity Hazard & Ground Worker Exclusion Zones
*Demo / Synthetic Operating Reference - Advisory Only*

## Exclusion Zone Dimensions
- **Critical Danger Zone**: 0.0m to 3.0m from machine tracks, counterweight, and bucket reach.
- **Warning Caution Zone**: 3.0m to 6.0m from machine perimeter.
- **Safe Transition Zone**: Greater than 6.0m.

## Immediate Action Protocol
1. When a ground worker enters the Critical Zone (<3.0m), the operator must immediately pause hydraulic movement, place the hydraulic lock lever in the locked position, and establish two-way radio or line-of-sight eye contact.
2. Under no circumstance should swing or trench digging continue while personnel are within the swing radius.
3. Proximity sensor alerts are advisory and do not replace physical spotters and daily site briefings.
