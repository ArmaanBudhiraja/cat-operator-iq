# Restraint Systems & ROPS Safety Protocol
*Demo / Synthetic Operating Reference - Advisory Only*

## Mandatory Compliance
1. Operators MUST fasten the 3-point retractable seatbelt prior to disengaging the hydraulic lockout lever or starting engine movement.
2. Rollover Protective Structures (ROPS) and Falling Object Protective Structures (FOPS) are engineered to protect the operator ONLY when secured inside the seat harness. In a tip-over, unbelted operators risk being pinned under the cab frame.
3. Unfastened seatbelt events trigger automated priority high safety alerts and log telemetry incidents for fleet review.
