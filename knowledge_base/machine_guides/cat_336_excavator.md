# CAT 336 Heavy Duty Hydraulic Excavator Operation Guide
*Demo / Synthetic Operating Reference - Advisory Only*

## Machine Overview
- Engine: CAT C9.3B ACERT, 314 HP (234 kW)
- Operating Weight: 37,200 kg
- Standard Bucket Capacity: 2.27 m³
- Maximum Digging Depth: 7,520 mm
- Hydraulic System Relief Pressure: 350 bar (5,075 psi)
- Operating Hydraulic Pressure (Nominal): 280 bar

## Daily Inspection Checklist
1. Inspect track tension and sprocket wear.
2. Check engine oil level and primary fuel filter water separator.
3. Verify hydraulic fluid level through sight glass with arm tucked and bucket resting flat on level ground.
4. Ensure swing bearing grease reservoir is replenished.

## Thermal & Vibration Limits
- Normal Operating Engine Temperature: 82°C – 95°C
- Warning Engine Temperature: 98°C – 104°C
- Critical Thermal Runaway: >= 105°C (Reduce engine load to low idle for 3 minutes before shutdown)
- Nominal Track/Boom Vibration Baseline: 1.5 – 2.0 mm/s
- Elevated Vibration Warning: > 2.8 mm/s
