# Mechanical Vibration & Structural Diagnostics
*Demo / Synthetic Operating Reference - Advisory Only*

## Vibration Root Causes
1. **Track Misalignment & Tension Loss**: Loose track chains induce periodic 2.5–3.5 mm/s vibrations during travel.
2. **Hydraulic Pump Cavitation**: Air ingress in suction line causes sharp high-frequency vibration and pressure fluctuations.
3. **Imbalanced Engine Harmonic Damper**: Causes baseline vibration to jump 30-50% across all RPM ranges.

## Threshold Actions
- **1.5 – 2.0 mm/s**: Normal operating vibration for heavy earthmoving machinery.
- **2.8 – 3.7 mm/s**: Elevated vibration. Schedule visual inspection of undercarriage and engine mounts at next break.
- **>= 3.8 mm/s**: Critical anomaly. Stop heavy excavation and conduct structural and hydraulic diagnostics.
